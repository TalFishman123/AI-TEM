UTEM calibration procedure:
turn on the beam, see a spot.
alignment is done from the top of the microscope to the bottom.

Illumination alignment:(everything here at minimum X40K magnification

spot 1 to spot 5: change spot# setting between 1 and 5. in spot 1 do gun alignment (XY control, in spot 5 change XY deflection to recenter. back and forth until spot center stays in place between 1 and 5.

shift and tilt: gun tilt to maximize current at working spot (usually 3)

column tilt: HT wobbler - looks like iris opening and closing off center of beam. XY control until it looks like it opens and closes around the center.

currently manual: open and close condenser aperture, move the aperture around until it is in the center of the beam.

Imaging:

move to diffraction mode and move to parallel beam with the condenser. change diffraction focus to sharpen the spot and then use some camera distance magnification.
two wobbler corrections. Diffraction mode to fix tilt until beam stops wobbling. Imaging(mag) mode to correct shift. two knobs for each.

Astigmatism:
fixed through condenser by changing brightness (L3 condenser focus).
